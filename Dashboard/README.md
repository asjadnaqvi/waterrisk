# European Inter-Regional Water Stress Explorer

An interactive view of blue-water consumption embodied in European regional supply chains in 2017. The map covers NUTS-2 regions in the EU27, Norway, and the United Kingdom.

## Coverage and interpretation

Regional imports represent water consumed to produce goods and services driven by demand in that region. Exports represent water consumed in the region to meet demand elsewhere. Flow arrows point from the water-use region to the region whose final demand drives that use. These are modeled, embodied-water relationships, not physical transfers of water.

The dashboard distinguishes direct and indirect contributions and source water-use sectors. Regions can be shaded by baseline water stress or by total imports or exports. Some extra-European sources contribute to import totals but are not mapped as regional flows.

This is an exploratory view of the 2017 blue-water-consumption analysis. It does not cover other years or indicators. Numerical results remain provisional pending comparison with the underlying analysis.
